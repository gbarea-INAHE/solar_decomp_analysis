# Data in this repository, and data that is not

| Data | In this repository? | Source and terms |
|---|---|---|
| `revision_v2/data/processed/*.csv` (124 tables) | **Yes** | Derived tables computed by the scripts: station-level statistics, bootstrap intervals, sensitivity and control checks. They contain aggregates, not hourly records of the providers. Licence: CC BY 4.0. They derive from the sources below; please also cite them. |
| `revision_v2/data/Mendoza_Datos_Medidos/*.csv` | **Yes** | One-minute global (`IRGLO`) and shadow-band-corrected diffuse (`IRDIF_C`) horizontal irradiance of the INAHE station in Mendoza, Argentina (32.89° S, 68.87° W), 2022–2025, in W/m². Empty cells are missing values. Eppley 8-48 pyranometers; the diffuse one with a home-built shadow band. Clock time UTC−3; the timestamp marks the end of each minute. Altitude not reported (750 m assumed in the analysis). Published with the authorisation of the data owner. Licence: CC BY 4.0. Please acknowledge Lic. Dario R. Jaime (CPA, INAHE-CONICET) and the INAHE station. |
| `revision_v2/data/ashrae_list/ashrae_arg_stations.csv` | **Yes** | Station list (name, WMO code, coordinates, elevation, time zone, Köppen class and data-quality flag) of the Argentine stations, extracted from the ASHRAE *IWEC2 Station List* (RP-1477 final report). Source URL and hash in `MANIFEST_ashrae_list.csv`. The PDF itself is not included. |
| `revision_v2/data/geo/ne_10m_admin_0_countries_arg.zip` | **Yes** | Natural Earth country outlines (public domain). |
| IWEC2 weather files (41 EPW files) | **No** | ASHRAE, *International Weather for Energy Calculations 2.0*. Not redistributed. Names, sizes and SHA-256 in `revision_v2/data/raw_manifest.csv`. |
| BSRN Petrolina and São Martinho da Serra, 2016 | **No** | Baseline Surface Radiation Network, through PANGAEA. Download with `scripts/download_anchor_pilot.py`; dataset DOIs and hashes in `revision_v2/data/anchor_raw/MANIFEST_anchor_raw.csv`. Cite each dataset as its PANGAEA record asks. |
| SURFRAD Desert Rock, 2016 | **No** | NOAA Global Monitoring Laboratory, SURFRAD network. Download with `scripts/download_anchor_pilot.py`. |
| SRRL Golden, 2021 (hourly) | **No** | NREL Solar Radiation Research Laboratory. Source and hash in `MANIFEST_anchor_raw.csv`. Check the provider's terms before reuse. |
| Hourly benchmark series (`hourly_v2.pkl.gz`) | **No** | Regenerated by `scripts/benchmark_v2.py` from the IWEC2 files; it contains their GHI, DNI and DHI. |

The licence of the derived tables does not change the terms of the providers' data they were computed from.
